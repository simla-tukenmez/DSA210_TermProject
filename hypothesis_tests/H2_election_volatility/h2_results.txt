================================================================================
RESEARCH QUESTION
================================================================================

Did the May 2023 Turkish elections cause significantly higher
volatility in BIST100 stock index returns?

================================================================================
HYPOTHESES
================================================================================

H0 (Null):        Variance(election) = Variance(non-election)
H2 (Alternative): Variance(election) > Variance(non-election)

================================================================================
METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

Comparison Periods:
  Election period:     May 1-31, 2023
  Non-election period: Jan-Apr, Jun-Dec 2023

Election Context:
  Round 1: May 14, 2023 (presidential election)
  Round 2: May 28, 2023 (runoff election)

Statistical Tests:
  Primary: Levene's test (robust to non-normality)
  Secondary: F-test for variance ratio

================================================================================
DESCRIPTIVE STATISTICS
================================================================================

Non-Election Period (2023, excluding May):
  Observations:  226
  Mean return:     0.0976%
  Std deviation:   2.3028%
  Variance:        5.3028
  Min:            -9.0125%
  Max:             9.4234%

Election Month (May 2023):
  Observations:  21
  Mean return:     0.2696%
  Std deviation:   3.0382%
  Variance:        9.2304
  Min:            -6.3355%
  Max:             7.5712%

Volatility Comparison:
  Variance ratio (Election/Non-Election): 1.7407
  → Election month was 74.1% MORE volatile

================================================================================
STATISTICAL TEST RESULTS
================================================================================

Levene's Test (Primary):
  Test statistic:         1.5792
  P-value (one-tailed):   0.1050
  Significance level:   α = 0.05

F-Test (Robustness Check):
  F-statistic:            1.7407
  P-value (one-tailed):   0.0287

================================================================================
CONCLUSION
================================================================================

Decision: FAIL TO REJECT H0 at α = 0.05

H2 is NOT SUPPORTED. The election month did NOT show significantly
higher volatility compared to non-election periods.

Although election volatility (σ = 3.0382%) differed from
non-election volatility (σ = 2.3028%), this difference
was not statistically significant (p = 0.1050).

================================================================================
INTERPRETATION
================================================================================

Political events like elections can increase market uncertainty,
leading to higher volatility. This test examined whether the
2023 Turkish presidential election created such volatility.

The lack of significant volatility increase may indicate:
1. Markets had already priced in election outcomes
2. Election results were not surprising to investors
3. Other factors dominated market volatility in 2023
