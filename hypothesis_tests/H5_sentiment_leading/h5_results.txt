RESEARCH QUESTION
================================================================================

Does Ekşi Sözlük sentiment have LEADING predictive power for
BIST100 returns? (i.e., does yesterday's sentiment predict today's returns?)

================================================================================
METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

Model: BIST100_Return[t] = β₀ + β₁ × Sentiment[t-1] + ε

Sentiment Source: Ekşi Sözlük daily entries (2023-2025)
  - BERT-based Turkish sentiment model
  - Daily aggregated scores (-1 to +1)

Sample Size: 698 observations

================================================================================
CORRELATION ANALYSIS
================================================================================

Same-day (Sentiment[t] ↔ Return[t]):  r =   0.1713
Leading (Sentiment[t-1] → Return[t]): r =   0.0032

→ Same-day correlation is STRONGER (reactive, not predictive)

================================================================================
REGRESSION RESULTS
================================================================================

  β₀ (Intercept):          0.1021
  β₁ (Sentiment[t-1]):     0.0229 
  P-value:                 0.9318
  R²:                      0.0000

================================================================================
CONCLUSION
================================================================================

Decision: H5 is NOT SUPPORTED

Sentiment does NOT have significant predictive power (p = 0.9318).
Leading correlation is essentially zero (r = 0.0032).

INTERPRETATION:
Ekşi Sözlük sentiment is REACTIVE rather than PREDICTIVE.
People express negative sentiment AFTER the market drops,
not before. Social media reflects rather than predicts market moves.
